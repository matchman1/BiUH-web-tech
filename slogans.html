Hardware Slogans

Brand lines from across the PC hardware world. Every one was checked against an official source — anything that could not be confirmed was left out.

ASUS

In Search of Incredible

The Chinese line 华硕品质，坚若磐石 dates from 2007 — note 坚若, not 坚如.

Global tagline

GIGABYTE

Upgrade Your Life

创新科技，美化人生

Stated company purpose, gigabyte.cn

MSI

Ultimate Computing, Infinite Potential

极致运算，无限潜能

Site headline, msi.cn

AMD

together we advance_

All lowercase, and the trailing underscore is part of the design — 同超越，共成就_

Brand refresh

Intel

That's the power of Intel Inside®

Unveiled 2 April 2025 at Intel Vision. The classic “Intel Inside” is still in use.

Intel Newsroom

ROG · Republic of Gamers

For Those Who Dare

“Republic of Gamers” is the brand name, not the slogan. Chinese: 只为超越

About ROG, rog.asus.com

Colorful · iGame

我游自在 —— 强大 不论现实与虚拟

Chinese-only. The brand also says 玩家至上. Its series carry their own lines — Vulcan is 智械觉醒.

Site headline, colorful.cn

Shengqi 圣旗

圣旗主板，年轻玩家的主板

Chinese-only, and the vendor explicitly labels it a slogan. An ASUS group brand since 2017.

shengqipc.cn

Corsair

PLAY TO YOUR LEVEL

Written in capitals on the official about page.

corsair.com/about

Western Digital

Unlock the Potential of Data

A mission statement rather than a stylised slogan, dated May 2022. WD split off SanDisk in 2025, so treat it as historical.

WD press release

Seagate

Leaders in Mass-Capacity Storage

A positioning line from the site, not a stylised slogan.

seagate.com

Samsung

Inspire the World, Create the Future

Declared in 2009 as the Vision 2020 corporate vision — not a current marketing line.

Announced at the company’s 40th anniversary

FSP 全汉

POWER NEVER ENDS

The official brand promise, written in capitals.

About FSP, fsp-group.com.tw

ASRock 华擎

Keep Leading

Used throughout its press releases. Its separate design philosophy is 设计创新、产品贴心、价格温馨 — a philosophy, not a slogan.

asrockchina.cn

Phanteks 追风者

不斷的追求突破　沒有不能完成的工作

Chinese-only company motto — “relentlessly pursue breakthroughs; no task is impossible.”

phanteks.com.tw

Cooler Master

Make It Yours

Its repositioning slogan from Computex 2015. Superseded since, kept here as history.

Computex 2015 coverage
