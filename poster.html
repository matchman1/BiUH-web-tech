OG image — 1200 × 630

  1. Press F12 to open DevTools
  2. In Elements, find <div class="poster" id="poster">
  3. Right-click it → Capture node screenshot
  4. Save the downloaded PNG as images/og-image.jpg

The box below is exactly 1200 × 630. If it is wider than your window, zoom out (Ctrl + -) — the capture is taken at full size regardless of zoom.
