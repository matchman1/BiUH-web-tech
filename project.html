CPU & GPU Generations

The chip generations behind modern PCs — one entry per architecture, oldest first, with a desktop flagship as the example. Intel, AMD and NVIDIA.

Intel Core

  1. 12th gen Alder Lake Nov 2021 Core i9-12900K
  2. 13th gen Raptor Lake Oct 2022 Core i9-13900K
  3. 14th gen Raptor Lake Refresh Oct 2023 Core i9-14900K
  4. Core Ultra 200S Arrow Lake Oct 2024 Core Ultra 9 285K
  5. Core Ultra 300 Panther Lake Jan 2026 Core Ultra X9 388H

AMD Ryzen

  1. Zen 3 Vermeer Nov 2020 Ryzen 9 5950X
  2. Zen 3D Vermeer-X Apr 2022 Ryzen 7 5800X3D
  3. Zen 4 Raphael Sep 2022 Ryzen 9 7950X
  4. Zen 5 Granite Ridge Aug 2024 Ryzen 9 9950X
  5. Zen 5 X3D Granite Ridge X3D Nov 2024 Ryzen 7 9800X3D

NVIDIA GeForce

  1. Maxwell GM204 Sep 2014 GeForce GTX 980
  2. Pascal GP102 May 2016 GeForce GTX 1080
  3. Turing TU102 Sep 2018 GeForce RTX 2080 Ti
  4. Ampere GA102 Sep 2020 GeForce RTX 3090
  5. Ada Lovelace AD102 Oct 2022 GeForce RTX 4090
  6. Blackwell GB202 Jan 2025 GeForce RTX 5090

Dates are launch dates, and the model listed is the flagship of that generation on desktop — not the only card in the line. A “generation” here means one architecture: the design every chip in that family is built from.
